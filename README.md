# Bible Reading — Support & Privacy Policy

Support for the **Designed to Last Bible Reading** iOS app.

## Privacy Policy

The Bible Reading app collects **no data** — nothing is tracked, logged, or transmitted. No accounts, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. Your reading progress is stored only on your device.

## Contact

For help, questions, or feedback, please [open an issue](https://github.com/dinopetrone/bible-reading-support/issues) on this repository.

## About the app

A one-year Bible reading plan with daily ESV Scripture passages, verse-by-verse commentary from Enduring Word (David Guzik), and a daily summary.
